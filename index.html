WHY IT MATTERS

Our Beach,
Our Responsibility.

Beaches are important ecosystems that support marine life, protect coastlines and provide valuable spaces for people. Protecting them is everyone's responsibility.

🐢

Protect Marine Life

Cleaner beaches create safer habitats for turtles, fish, seabirds and countless marine organisms.

🌱

Healthy Ecosystems

Coastal ecosystems help support biodiversity and maintain the natural balance of our oceans.

🌍

Responsible choices today can help future generations enjoy cleaner, healthier and safer beaches.

What Is Happening
to Our Beaches?

Human activities can have a significant impact on coastal environments and marine ecosystems.

🧴

Plastic Pollution

Plastic bottles, bags, packaging and other waste can remain in the environment for many years.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Simple Ways to
Protect the Beach.

♻️

Reduce Plastic

Choose reusable bottles, bags and containers whenever possible.

🗑️

Dispose Waste Properly

Use appropriate bins and recycle materials whenever facilities are available.

🐢

Respect Wildlife

Keep a safe distance from marine animals, nests and sensitive coastal habitats.

🧹

Join Beach Cleanups

Participate in community activities and encourage others to protect the coastline.
